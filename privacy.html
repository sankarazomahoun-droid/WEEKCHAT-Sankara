Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Weekchat is a student community app published by ZOMAHOUN Sankara. Contact: sankarazomahoun@gmail.com, +229 0191646137.

Data we collect

  • Profile: name, university, field, year, description, photo (optional).
  • Content you post: text, photos, videos, audio, comments, lessons, exercises.
  • Private messages: encrypted by the app before they are sent.
  • Your account email and the technical data needed to run the service.
  • Your reminders, tasks, calendar and timetable: stored on your device only.

Why we use it

To run the app: show your profile and posts, allow conversations, and keep the community safe through moderation.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is hosted by Supabase (servers in Ireland). When you use the study assistant, your question is sent to Anthropic to produce an answer.

Age

Weekchat is for people aged 13 and older. If we learn that a user is under 13, the account is deleted.

Safety

You can report content and block users. Accounts that break the community rules can be removed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to the email above.

Delete your account

To delete your Weekchat account and your data:

  1. Send an email to sankarazomahoun@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Weekchat account”.
  2. Write from the email address you used to sign up, and include your full name.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: your profile, posts, comments, likes, messages and uploaded files.

Some information may be kept only if the law requires it.
