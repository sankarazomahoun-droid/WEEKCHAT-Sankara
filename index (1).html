Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Weekchat is a student community app published by ZOMAHOUN Sankara. Contact: sankarazomahoun@gmail.com, +229 0191646137.

Data we collect

  • Profile: name, university, field, year, description, photo (optional).
  • Content you post: text, photos, videos, comments, lessons, exercises.
  • Private messages: encrypted by the app before sending.
  • Your account email and technical data needed to run the service.
  • Your reminders, tasks, calendar and timetable: stored on your device only.

Why

To run the app: show your profile and posts, allow conversations, moderation and safety.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is hosted by Supabase (servers in Ireland). When you use the study assistant, your question is sent to Anthropic to produce an answer.

Deletion

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by writing to the email above. An in-app delete button is coming.

Age

Weekchat is for people aged 13 and older. If we learn that a user is under 13, the account is deleted.

Safety

You can report content and block users. Accounts that break the rules can be removed.
